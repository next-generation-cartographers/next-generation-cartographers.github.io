date: 2026-06-05
---

We are excited to announce the start of our NGC initiative: [**NGC Map Tutorials**](/resources/map-tutorials/), a free and accessible tutorial library for map-makers, where you can contribute!

For example:

- How to apply a water texture?
- How do I evaluate the quality of a dataset?
- What is an appropriate way to label Indigenous lands?

There are already existing blogs that list resources and tutorials related to map-making. But our tutorial library is unique by focusing on:

- **Bottom-up approach:** You define the topics. The library will reflect the topics on which you are interested and skilled.
- **Accessible language:** The tutorials should be accessible to anyone making maps. That means, for example, that they are written in an easy English language (understandable by non-native speakers) and non-academic language.
- **Applied knowledge:** The tutorials provide knowledge that can be applied by the readers.
- **The target group is cartographers and non-cartographers who make maps:** That means, people who are not necessarily familiar with cartography theory and principles, but who make maps.

And anyone can write a tutorial! Once published, we will advertise it on our platforms. The process is easy, you will find all the details on our [how-to-contribute page](/resources/map-tutorials/how-to/write-a-map-tutorial/). We encourage any perspective on any map-related topic to be submitted.

Let us know if you have any questions or comments. We are looking forward to receiving your tutorials!
