Welcome to the NGC’s #30DayMapChallenge! 
Last year we started a fun experiment: we joined forces within our NGC community 
for the [#30DayMapChallenge](https://30daymapchallenge.com/) ✨🗺️ 
Honestly, we think it was great fun ([see here for a visual recap](../resources/30daymapchallenge/2025/)), 
so this year we’re planning to do it again. 
The idea is the following: You sign up for a day/topic (or a couple of them), 
you create an awesome map, post it on LinkedIn, and we repost your map. Done! 🙌

## So what’s the process?

[You can sign up for a topic here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Y2iZDXrTZwhhjxquTD22J3apfK28NRqutEUNqyyH4W0/edit?usp=sharing).
In the 2nd sheet of this document, there’s a list where you can enter the date and your name; 
the topic will fill automatically. 
If there’s already someone else who will create a map for this day – no problem. 
It’s not the goal to have exactly one map per day, or even to tick a box for each of the 30 days. 
**The main goal is to create maps, have fun while doing so, support each other, and learn from each other.** 
So just sign up for the day(s) you’re most interested in 🙂 
On the Overview sheet, you can see all the topics and who already signed up. 

On the day of your map, post your map on LinkedIn and tag us 
([@Next Generation Cartographers](https://www.linkedin.com/company/next-generation-cartographers/)), 
so we can repost your map.

If you feel like it, you can also add [our NGC logo](../resources/branding/) 
to your map (no obligation!).

## And what about the community?
We aim to make the #30DayMapChallenge an enjoyable team effort 
(instead of a perhaps scary solo marathon). 
To do so, we have two things in place:
- A [**dedicated Discord channel**](https://discord.gg/eCD5hHCkF) where you can get feedback, motivation, and perhaps ideas if you’re stuck
- A [**community meet-up on December 3rd**](https://tuwien.zoom.us/meeting/register/qAse5S90Q3ysP074p9mnjw) to meet online and share with each other what we have achieved together during this year’s challenge.

## FAQ

### Someone already signed-up for the day/topic I wanted to submit a map. What should I do?

It’s totally fine to have multiple people signed up for the same day/topic. 
Simply copy the respective row in the google sheet and add your name! That’s it. 
We will make sure to repost all submissions on LinkedIn. 

### Are there any requirements for the maps I want to submit?
There is only one, but it is important: 
all the maps need to adhere to [our Code of Conduct](https://github.com/next-generation-cartographers/ngc-code-of-conduct/blob/main/code-of-conduct.pdf).

### I want to get feedback and/or share my maps or the process. Where can I do this?
That’s exactly what our dedicated Discord channel is for. 
Join our Discord server and find 30DayMapChallenge enthusiasts in our [#30day-map-challenge channel](https://discord.gg/eCD5hHCkF). 
This is also where all communication from our side about the map challenge will take place, 
so it’s highly recommended to join and check for updates frequently.

### What happens to the maps I publish on LinkedIn for the NGC 30DayMapChallenge?
We will repost them from our NGC LinkedIn account, 
and we will publish them on our Website, similar to [last year’s maps](https://next-generation-cartographers.github.io/resources/30daymapchallenge/2025/). 
If you do not want your maps to be published on our website, 
please inform us by 30 November 2026 by emailing [ester.scheck@geo.tuwien.ac.at](mailto:ester.scheck@geo.tuwien.ac.at).
