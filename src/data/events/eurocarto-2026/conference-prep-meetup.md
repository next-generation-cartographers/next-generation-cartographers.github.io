Our Online Community Meet-Up #15 will get you ready for EuroCarto!

Join us to get all important information directly from the local organizers. They and people who have been to EuroCarto before will answer all your questions.

You will also get the opportunity to try out your presentation or share your poster draft and get feedback from the community. 
Your work does not have to be finished, you can show in progress, you can share just a general structure or an idea for a presentation start, or you can do your full presentation.Contributions will be organised on the spot, so just come with your work. 

The meet-up will take max. 1h 15 minutes, depending on the number of contributions.

And even if you won't be at EuroCarto, this meet-up is for you, because you can get insights into a variety of topics that will be presented and discussed at the conference.
